📰 News Express

Privacy Policy

News Express ("the App") delivers short, 60-word news summaries in multiple languages including Hindi, English, Marathi, Gujarati, and more. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information while you use the App.

Information We Collect

  • Language preference: The language you choose (Hindi, English, Marathi, Gujarati, etc.) is stored so the App can show news in your preferred language.
  • Favorite & saved stories: Stories you mark as favorite are stored so you can access them later.
  • Reading activity: Basic usage data such as which stories you view, swipe through, or delete may be used to improve your news feed.
  • Device information: Standard device and app information (device type, OS version) may be collected for performance and troubleshooting.

How We Use Your Information

  • To show news summaries in your selected language.
  • To personalize your feed as you swipe up for new news.
  • To manage your favorite stories and let you delete stories you no longer want saved.
  • To improve app performance and content relevance over time.

Swipe & Feed Feature

The swipe-up feature loads new 60-word news stories continuously. Your swipe activity may be used only to determine which stories to show next and is not shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

Favorite Stories

When you add a story to favorites, it is saved either on your device or securely on our servers (if you are signed in) so you can revisit it anytime. You can remove any story from favorites whenever you like.

Delete Story

When you delete a saved or favorite story, it is permanently removed from your list and is no longer accessible within the App.

Share Story

When you use the "Share" option, the story link or content is passed to the app you choose (such as WhatsApp, messaging, or social media) through your device's standard sharing tools. News Express does not access your contacts or messages during this process.

View Full Story (Web View)

Tapping "View Full Story" opens the complete article in an in-app web view, redirecting you to the original publisher's website. Please note that the publisher's own privacy policy will apply to any data collected on their site.

Permissions

  • Internet access: Required to fetch and display news stories.
  • Storage access: Used to save your favorite stories and language preferences locally.
  • No access to contacts, camera, or microphone is requested by the App.

Third-Party Services

News Express may use third-party news providers and analytics tools to deliver content and improve app performance. These providers are bound to handle data securely and are not permitted to use it for unrelated purposes.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Children's Privacy

News Express is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
