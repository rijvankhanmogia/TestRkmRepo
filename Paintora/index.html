Privacy Policy

Paintora ("the App") is a simple painting app that lets you draw with multiple colors, open images, fill areas with color, and save or share your paintings. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

Paintora is designed to work with minimal data collection:

  • Images you open: When you open an image to paint on, it is processed only on your device and is not uploaded to any server.
  • Paintings you create: Your artwork is saved locally on your device unless you choose to share it.
  • No account required: Paintora does not require sign-up, login, or personal account information.

How We Use Your Information

Since Paintora processes images and paintings locally on your device, we do not collect, store, or transmit your creations to our servers. Your paintings remain private unless you actively choose to share them.

Sharing Feature

When you use the "Share" option, your painting is passed to apps you select (such as messaging or social media apps) through your device's standard sharing tools. Paintora does not access or store the content once you share it.

Permissions

  • Photo/Storage Access: Used only to let you open an image to paint on or save your finished painting.
  • No location, contacts, or microphone access is requested by the App.

Data Storage

All paintings and opened images are stored locally on your device. Paintora does not upload your content to any cloud service or third-party server.

Third-Party Services

Paintora does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Paintora does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App is safe for users of all ages since it does not collect personal data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
