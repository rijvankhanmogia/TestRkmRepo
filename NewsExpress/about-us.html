About us

NewsExpress · Published by Indian AI

NewsExpress condenses the day's reporting into 60-word summaries so you can stay informed in the time it takes to finish your chai. Swipe through headlines, save what you want to return to, and open the original article whenever a story deserves the full read.

How NewsExpress works

NewsExpress is a news aggregator. We do not employ reporters and we do not write the underlying articles. Each summary is generated from reporting published by third-party news organisations, and every story in the app and on this website names its publisher, shows its publication date, and links directly to the original article on the publisher's own site.

All copyright and other rights in the source articles remain with the publishers concerned. If you are a publisher and would like a story removed, please contact us and we will act promptly.

What you can do

  • Read in 60 words — every story trimmed to the essentials.
  • Choose your language — English, Hindi, Marathi and Gujarati.
  • Save stories — keep anything worth revisiting.
  • Share — send a story with its source and link to friends.
  • Breaking-news alerts — in the Android app.
