Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

This policy explains what the NewsExpress Android app and this website collect, why, and what choices you have. NewsExpress is published by Indian AI and developed by Mohd Rijvan Khan Mogia, Maharashtra Nagar, Mumbai, Maharashtra — 400088, India.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts. We do not sell personal information, and we do not build advertising profiles about you ourselves.

2. Information stored on your device

These stay on your own device and are never uploaded to us:

  • Your language choice, so the feed opens in the language you prefer.
  • Saved stories, kept locally so your favourites list works offline.
  • App settings, such as how many stories you have viewed between adverts, and whether you have seen the introduction.

Clearing the app's storage, or your browser's site data for this website, removes all of it.

3. News content and third-party publishers

NewsExpress is a news aggregator. Summaries are derived from articles published by third-party news organisations, and each story names its publisher and links to the original article. When you open an original article, you leave NewsExpress and that publisher's own privacy policy applies. We have no control over, and take no responsibility for, the content or practices of those sites.

4. Notifications

The Android app can send breaking-news notifications. On Android 13 and later, these are only sent if you grant notification permission, and you can withdraw it at any time in your device settings. Notifications are generated from the public news feed — they do not involve any personal data.

5. Advertising

The Android app shows adverts supplied by Google AdMob. Google may use device identifiers, including the advertising ID, to serve and measure adverts. This processing is carried out by Google as an independent provider, and is governed by Google's advertising policies.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of personalised advertising, from Settings → Google → Ads on your Android device. We may also display our own adverts for local businesses; these do not track you.

6. Analytics and hosting

App configuration, such as which languages are available, is fetched from Google Firebase. As with any internet request, the server receives your device's IP address and a standard user-agent string in order to respond. We do not use this to identify you.

7. Children

NewsExpress reports general news and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you on our servers, there is normally nothing for us to export or erase. If you believe we hold information about you, or you want a story or image removed, email rijvankhanmogia@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Security

All news and configuration requests are made over encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the limited information involved.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app develops. Material changes will be reflected by the "last updated" date above, and where appropriate announced in the app.
