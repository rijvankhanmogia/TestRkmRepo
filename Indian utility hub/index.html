01Who this policy is from

Indian Utility Hub is published on Google Play by INDIAN AI and developed by Mohd Rijvan Khan Mogia. The policy covers the Android application with package name rkm.app.indianutilityhub, and applies to every screen and tool inside it.

In this policy, "the app" means Indian Utility Hub and "you" means the person using it.

02What the app does

Indian Utility Hub bundles 29 offline tools: GST, EMI, SIP, simple and compound interest, percentage and discount calculators; a PIN code helper and lakh/crore number formatting; 7 unit converters; age, date difference, working days and countdown tools; a word counter, case converter and password generator.

Every one of these runs on your device using the numbers you type. No calculation is sent to a server, because there is no server.

03Information the app does not collect

The app does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. Specifically, it does not ask for or gather:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any account details — there is no sign-up or login.
  • Your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files.
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs.
  • Usage analytics, behavioural tracking or crash reports.

No advertising networks, analytics services or third-party tracking software are built into the app.

04Information stored on your device

So the app is useful across sessions, a few things are saved locally in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • Calculation history — the inputs and results of tools you use, with the date, so you can revisit them later.
  • Favourites — the tools you mark for quick access.
  • Preferences — such as your light or dark theme choice.

This information never leaves your device. It is not readable by other apps, it is not backed up to any service run by the developer, and the developer has no way to see it.

05Deleting your data

You stay in control of everything the app saves:

  • Delete a single entry from the History screen at any time.
  • Clear the entire history from the app's settings.
  • Use Android's Settings → Apps → Indian Utility Hub → Storage → Clear data to wipe everything at once.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its stored data from your device permanently.

Because nothing is stored outside your phone, there is no copy left behind for anyone to delete on your behalf.

06Permissions and internet access

The app is designed to work fully offline and does not require any sensitive Android permission to function. It does not read your storage, location, contacts or call logs.

If a future version needs a new permission or an internet connection for a specific feature, that will be explained inside the app and this policy will be updated before the feature ships.

07Children's privacy

Indian Utility Hub is a general-purpose calculator and converter app and is safe for all ages. Since it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects no personal information from children under 13 either. If you believe a child has somehow shared personal data through the app, please write to the address in section 11 and it will be investigated and removed.

08Security

Your data stays inside the app's private sandbox on your device, protected by Android's own security model and by your device lock. Keeping your phone updated and locked with a PIN, pattern or biometric is the most effective way to protect the history saved in the app.

09Third parties and Google Play

The app itself connects to no third-party service. Distribution is handled by Google Play, and Google may collect its own information when you download or update the app — that is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, not by this one.

This policy matches the Data safety declaration submitted on the Google Play Console for this app.

10Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date at the top will change. Significant changes will also be noted in the release notes on Google Play. Reviewing this page occasionally is the best way to stay informed.
