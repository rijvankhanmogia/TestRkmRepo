Our Story

How Alfalah Academy Began

Founded in 2011 by a group of educators and well-wishers from the Sultana community, Alfalah Academy started with just three classrooms and a firm belief — that quality education should not be a privilege of the cities alone. What began with a handful of eager students has today grown into a thriving school serving families across Chirawa tehsil and neighbouring villages of Jhunjhunu district.

Over the years we have steadily expanded — adding smart classrooms, science and computer laboratories, a well-stocked library, and safe transport. Yet our founding spirit remains unchanged: to combine a strong academic curriculum with sports, arts and moral values, so that every child leaves us not only educated, but ready for life.
