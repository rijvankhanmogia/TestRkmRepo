How We Help Everyday India

Android App Development

We build native Android applications that are fast, lightweight, and designed for Indian network conditions and budget devices. Published on Google Play Store under the IndianAI account.

  • Optimized for low-end Android devices
  • Data-efficient for slower connections
  • Small APK sizes for easy downloads
  • Regular updates and maintenance

News & Content Apps

Our NewsExpress app delivers the latest news summarized in 60 words or fewer. We focus on clarity and brevity so users can stay informed without spending excessive time reading.

  • 60-word news summaries
  • Multiple news categories
  • Fast loading even on slow internet
  • Clean, distraction-free reading experience

Offline Utility Tools

IndianUtilityHub provides 29 daily-use tools that work completely offline. From financial calculators to unit converters, we cover the tools Indians use every day.

  • EMI & loan calculators
  • Simple & compound interest
  • Age, BMI, unit converters
  • GST, discount, percentage tools

AI-Powered Features

InshortAI uses Google Gemini to give users quick, accurate answers in 60 words. We bring AI capabilities to everyday users in a simple, accessible format.

  • Google Gemini integration
  • Concise 60-word AI answers
  • Simple question-and-answer interface
  • Everyday practical use cases

Document Management Tools

DocSnap provides 21 document tools including image-to-PDF conversion, PDF merging, PDF splitting, and more. All essential document operations in one place.

  • Image to PDF conversion
  • Merge & split PDFs
  • PDF to image extraction
  • Compress and rotate PDFs

Games & Entertainment

VillageCross is a 4-player strategy board game on a 5x5 grid, bringing traditional Indian gaming entertainment to mobile. Paintora adds creative painting and craft tools for artistic users.

  • Multiplayer board game
  • Traditional game mechanics
  • Painting and craft tools
  • Creative expression tools

Sports & Community

CrickAdda is India's local cricket companion. Follow live scores, stream matches, chat in groups, and build fantasy teams — all focused on local and community cricket.

  • Local cricket live scores
  • Group chat for fans
  • Fantasy team builder
  • Community-focused features

  • Clear privacy policies
