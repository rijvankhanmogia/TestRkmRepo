What is NewsExpress?

NewsExpress is an Android news application built to solve a simple problem: most people want to stay informed but don't have the time to read lengthy articles throughout the day.

Our approach is straightforward — every news story is condensed to approximately 60 words, giving you just enough context to understand what happened, why it matters, and what comes next. No fluff. No filler. Just the essential information.

Whether you're commuting, on a lunch break, or have just a few spare minutes, NewsExpress ensures you never miss an important story. The app covers six major categories: India, World, Sports, Business, Technology, and Entertainment — so there's always something relevant for you.

Why We Built NewsExpress

The idea for NewsExpress came from a very personal observation: most people scroll past the same headlines repeatedly without actually reading the articles because they're too long. The average news article takes 4–7 minutes to read. Multiply that by 10 stories a day and you've spent over an hour just catching up on news.

We asked: what if we could deliver the core information of any news event in the time it takes to read a text message? The answer was NewsExpress.

By summarizing each story to approximately 60 words, we help users stay genuinely informed — not just scroll-informed. You learn what happened, why it matters, and what to expect next, all before your tea gets cold.

This app is built for the everyday Indian reader — the student, the professional, the parent — who cares about the world around them but has a life to live too.
