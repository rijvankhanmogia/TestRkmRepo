Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Read how we collect, use, and protect your information.

App Name: Paintora  |  Developer / Operator: Mohd Rijvan Khan Mogia (IndianAI)  |  Last Updated: September 2026

1 Introduction

Welcome to Paintora ("the App," "we," "us," or "our"). Paintora is a digital painting and creativity application developed and operated by Mohd Rijvan Khan Mogia under the IndianAI platform, published on the Google Play Store.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use our App. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a transparent relationship with our users.

By downloading, installing, or using Paintora, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of the App.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us using the details provided at the end of this document.

2 Information We May Collect

Paintora is primarily a creative and offline-capable app. We aim to collect as little personal data as possible. However, the following types of information may be collected or processed depending on how you interact with the App:

2.1 Information You Provide
  • Artwork and creative content you create within the App (stored locally on your device unless you choose to share or export it).
  • Contact information you voluntarily submit if you reach out to us for support or feedback (name, email address, message content).
2.2 Information Collected Automatically
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information may be collected automatically by third-party services integrated into the App.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the App, such as features used, session duration, crash reports, and performance metrics, may be collected by analytics services.
  • Log Data: In the event of an error or crash, the App may collect log data including your device's IP address, device name, operating system version, configuration of the App, and the time and date of your use.
2.3 Information We Do Not Collect
  • We do not require you to create an account to use the core features of Paintora.
  • We do not knowingly collect financial information, payment details, or sensitive personal data.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or location unless you have expressly granted such permission for a specific feature that requires it.

3 How We Use Information

The information we collect is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Paintora application and its features.
  • To understand how users interact with the App in order to enhance the user experience.
  • To respond to your enquiries, feedback, or support requests.
  • To diagnose technical issues, crashes, and errors and improve app stability.
  • To monitor and analyse usage trends and activity patterns within the App.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to third parties for marketing or advertising purposes without your explicit consent.

Paintora is distributed via the Google Play Store and may use Google Play Services for app distribution, in-app billing, and platform-level features. Google's Privacy Policy governs the data collected through these services. Please review Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4.2 Firebase / Google Analytics (if applicable)

The App may use Firebase Analytics or similar analytics services to collect anonymous usage data. This data helps us understand how users interact with the App, which features are most popular, and where improvements can be made. Analytics data is collected in aggregate and is not tied to your personal identity.

4.3 Crash Reporting (if applicable)

Crash reporting tools may be used to capture error data when the App experiences a crash or unexpected behaviour. This data is used solely to identify and fix bugs and improve app stability.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services used in connection with the App, as their practices may differ from ours.

5 Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we collect against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include standard industry practices for securing data in transit and at rest.

However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. We encourage you to take appropriate precautions to protect your personal information, including keeping your device secure.

In the event of a data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will make reasonable efforts to notify affected parties in accordance with applicable law.

6 Children's Privacy

Paintora is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us without your consent, please contact us immediately using the contact details below. Upon verification, we will take reasonable steps to promptly remove such information from our records.

If we discover that personal data from a child under the relevant age of consent has been collected, we will delete that data as quickly as reasonably possible.

7 Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as is necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable laws and regulations.

Artwork and creative content you generate in the App is stored locally on your device and is subject to your own device management. We do not upload or store your artwork on our servers unless you explicitly use a sharing or cloud-backup feature.

If you contact us for support or feedback, we may retain your contact information and message content for up to 12 months to facilitate any follow-up communications and to improve our services.

You may request deletion of any personal data we hold by contacting us using the information provided in Section 10.
