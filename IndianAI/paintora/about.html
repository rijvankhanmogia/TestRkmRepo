What Makes Paintora Special

Every feature is designed to elevate your creative experience

Pencil Pen Brush Eraser

Professional Brush Engine

Paintora features a robust brush engine with multiple stroke types. Each brush is carefully calibrated to mimic real-world art tools, providing natural pressure sensitivity and smooth stroke rendering.

Adjust brush size, opacity, and blending mode on the fly. Whether you are sketching a rough idea or adding fine details to a finished piece, the brush engine delivers precision and control.

Non-Destructive Layer System

Work with multiple independent layers, just like professional desktop software. Experiment freely — draw, paint, and edit without affecting other parts of your artwork.

Add new layers, reorder them with drag-and-drop, adjust layer opacity, and merge layers when you are done. The layer system empowers complex compositions and detailed artwork.

Rich Color & Palette System

Access thousands of preset colors organised into warm, cool, pastel, and vibrant categories. The color wheel lets you dial in any custom shade with precision.

Save your favourite colors to a personal palette for quick access. The eyedropper tool lets you sample any color from your canvas instantly — perfect for maintaining consistency.
