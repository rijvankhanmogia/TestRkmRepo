Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Most tools are offline — no data is collected or shared.

Last Updated: September 2026 Developer: Mohd Rijvan Khan Mogia App: IndianUtilityHub
Quick Summary: IndianUtilityHub is a primarily offline app. We do not collect, store, or share your personal information. All calculations are performed locally on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how IndianUtilityHub ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Mohd Rijvan Khan Mogia and published on the Google Play Store under the IndianAI platform, handles information in connection with the use of our Android mobile application.

By downloading, installing, or using IndianUtilityHub, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the application.

2. Information We Collect

IndianUtilityHub is designed with a privacy-first, offline-first philosophy. The vast majority of our 29 tools operate entirely on your device and require no internet connection or data transmission of any kind.

2.1 Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Financial information entered into calculators (EMI, SIP, PPF, FD, loan amounts)
  • Health data entered (BMI calculations, weight, height)
  • Location data or GPS information
  • Device contacts, camera, or microphone data
  • Browsing history or usage patterns
  • Cookies or tracking identifiers

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you use IndianUtilityHub, the Google Play Store may automatically collect certain anonymized technical information as part of their standard platform analytics (such as crash reports, install counts, and device compatibility data). This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's own privacy policy. We have no access to personally identifiable information through these channels.

3. Offline Nature of the App

IndianUtilityHub's core offering — all 29 calculation and conversion tools — functions completely offline. This means:

  • No data is sent to any server when you perform a calculation.
  • All results are computed locally on your device's processor.
  • Values you enter are not stored permanently — they reset when you close the tool.
  • The app does not require an active internet connection to function (except for initial download).
  • Currency conversion uses offline rates bundled within the app and does not make live API calls.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Since we do not collect personal data, there is nothing to share. However, we want to be transparent:

  • Third Parties: We do not sell, trade, rent, or share any user data with third parties.
  • Google Play Services: The app is distributed via Google Play, which may have its own data practices. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for details.
  • Legal Requirements: In the unlikely event we are required by law to disclose any information, we will comply with applicable legal obligations.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of the app, users will be notified of any changes to this Privacy Policy.

5. App Permissions

IndianUtilityHub requests minimal device permissions. The following explains any permissions that may be requested and why:

  • No Permission Required for Core Tools: All 29 calculators and converters function without any special device permissions.
  • Storage (optional): If a future version includes export-to-PDF or share-result functionality, storage permission may be requested solely to save the exported file to your device.
  • Internet: Not required for core functionality. May be requested only for optional update checks.

We will always explain why a permission is needed at the time it is requested, and you can deny any optional permission without losing access to core functionality.

6. Children's Privacy

IndianUtilityHub does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. The app is a general-purpose utility tool suitable for users of all ages. Since we do not collect any personal data from any user, there are no special risks to children's privacy.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has somehow provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

7. Data Security

Because IndianUtilityHub does not transmit any user data over the internet (for core functions), the risk of a data breach is virtually eliminated for normal usage. Any data you enter into the app's tools stays on your device and is cleared when you exit.

We recommend keeping your Android device up to date with security patches to ensure overall device security. We also recommend downloading IndianUtilityHub exclusively from the official Google Play Store to avoid tampered or malicious versions.

8. Third-Party Services

IndianUtilityHub does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking libraries within the app. This means:

  • No advertisements are shown in the app.
  • No analytics tools (such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.) are embedded in the current version.
  • No third-party social media login is required.

The only third-party service indirectly involved is the Google Play Store for app distribution. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy for details on their data practices.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be reflected with a new "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Significant changes will be communicated through an in-app notification or the app's Play Store listing. Continued use of IndianUtilityHub after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Your Rights

Since IndianUtilityHub does not collect personal data, there is generally no personal data to access, correct, delete, or port. However, if you believe any data has been inadvertently collected, you have the right to:

  • Request confirmation of whether any personal data is held.
  • Request deletion of any data that may have been collected.
  • Withdraw consent (by uninstalling the app).

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details provided in Section 10.
