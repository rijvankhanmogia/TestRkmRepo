The App

What is IndianUtilityHub?

IndianUtilityHub is a free Android application offering 29 offline daily-use tools designed specifically for Indians. Whether you're a student calculating percentages, a professional estimating EMI or GST, an investor planning SIP or PPF, or simply someone wanting a quick BMI check — this app has you covered.

The app works entirely without an internet connection. Every tool is available the moment you open the app, with no sign-up, no ads, and no data collection. It is designed to be fast, lightweight, and easy to use for people of all age groups.

Mission

Our Mission

IndianUtilityHub was created with one simple goal: to provide a comprehensive, reliable, and completely free toolkit to every Indian — regardless of internet connectivity.

In a country where millions of users face intermittent internet access, we believe every essential calculation tool should work offline, out of the box, without any cost or compromise.

From EMI calculations for home loans to GST breakdowns for small business owners, from SIP planning for investors to BMI checks for health-conscious individuals — IndianUtilityHub empowers everyone.
