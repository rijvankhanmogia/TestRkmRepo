Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. Here's exactly how InshortAI handles your data.

Last Updated: October 2026
Summary: InshortAI uses Google Gemini AI to answer your questions — your queries are sent to Google. The app also uses Google AdMob for advertising, which collects the Android Advertising ID. An anonymous device identifier (Android ID) is used to manage app functionality via Firebase. Your question history is stored only on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how InshortAI ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Mohd Rijvan Khan Mogia under the IndianAI platform, handles information when you use our mobile application ("App"). By downloading or using InshortAI, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Our app is designed with a privacy-first approach: we collect the minimum data necessary for the app to function and we do not build profiles or sell data.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

InshortAI does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use the app. The only information you voluntarily provide is:

  • Questions / Queries: Text or voice-transcribed text that you enter to ask InshortAI.
  • Google Gemini API Key: If you provide an API key, it is stored locally on your device using Android's encrypted SharedPreferences. It is never transmitted to our servers.

2.2 Automatically Collected — Device or Other IDs

InshortAI reads your device's Android ID (Settings.Secure.ANDROID_ID) to create an anonymous user identifier. This identifier is transmitted to and stored in Firebase Realtime Database (a Google service) for app functionality purposes such as managing usage credits or session data. This Android ID is:

  • Collected: Yes — read from your device automatically when the app starts.
  • Shared: Yes — transmitted to Firebase Realtime Database (Google) as the user key.
  • Purpose: App functionality (user identity / session management).
  • Not linked to your name, email, or other personal identity.

This data type is declared in our Google Play Data Safety declaration as: "Device or other IDs — Collected and Shared — App functionality".

2.3 Automatically Collected — Advertising ID

InshortAI displays advertisements via Google AdMob (Google LLC). The AdMob SDK automatically collects your device's Android Advertising ID for ad personalisation and measurement. This Advertising ID is:

  • Collected: Yes — automatically by the AdMob SDK.
  • Shared: Yes — with Google's advertising network for ad targeting and measurement.
  • Purpose: Advertising or marketing.

This data type is declared in our Google Play Data Safety declaration as: "Device or other IDs — Advertising ID — Collected and Shared — Advertising or marketing".

You can opt out of personalised ads at any time: go to Android Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation. You can also reset your Advertising ID from the same menu.

2.4 Local Storage on Your Device

InshortAI stores the following locally on your device only:

  • Your question history (the questions you have asked and their answers)
  • Your Gemini API key (encrypted)
  • App settings and preferences (e.g., dark mode toggle)

This data is stored in your device's local storage and is never transmitted to our servers. You can clear this data at any time by clearing the app's data from your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

3. Google Gemini API — Third-Party Data Processing

This is an important section that you should read carefully.

InshortAI uses the Google Gemini API to generate AI responses. When you submit a question in the app, your question text is sent directly from your device to Google's Gemini API servers for processing. This means:

  • Your questions are transmitted to Google via the Gemini API.
  • Google may process, store, and use your queries in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms of service.
  • We (InshortAI / IndianAI) do not receive, intercept, or store these queries on our servers.
  • The answers generated are returned directly from Google to your device.
Important: By using InshortAI, you acknowledge and agree that your questions will be processed by Google under the Google Gemini API Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy. We strongly recommend reviewing:

• Google Privacy Policy
• Google Generative AI Terms of Service

3.1 Google's Data Handling

According to Google's documentation, data submitted to the Gemini API may be used by Google to improve their AI models, subject to their opt-out policies. The developer (InshortAI / IndianAI) has no control over Google's data processing practices. For the most current information, please refer directly to Google's privacy documentation.

4. Microphone and Voice Input

If you use the Voice Input feature, InshortAI will request access to your device's microphone. This permission is used solely to transcribe your spoken question into text. Audio is processed locally using Android's built-in speech recognition service (Google Speech-to-Text). We do not record, store, or transmit raw audio data.

You may revoke microphone permission at any time through your device's app permission settings. The app will continue to function normally using text input.

5. What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not sell your personal data to any third party for their independent use.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, files, calendar, or other device data.
  • We do not create personal profiles beyond anonymous session management.
  • We do not store your questions or answers on our own servers.
  • We do not generate images, videos, or documents. InshortAI is strictly a question-and-answer application providing concise text responses of approximately 60 words.

Note: InshortAI does display advertisements via Google AdMob and does transmit an anonymous device identifier to Firebase. See Sections 2.2, 2.3, and 7 for full details.

6. Children's Privacy

InshortAI is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information to us, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate action.

Users aged 13–17 should use the app under parental supervision, particularly in relation to the Google Gemini API's own terms regarding age requirements.

7. Third-Party Services

InshortAI integrates the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data:

7.1 Google Gemini API

Used to generate AI answers. Your question text is sent to Google's servers. Governed by Google Privacy Policy and Gemini API Terms.

7.2 Firebase Realtime Database (Google)

Used for app functionality. The app transmits your device's Android ID to Firebase Realtime Database as an anonymous user key. Firebase is operated by Google LLC. Governed by Firebase Privacy Policy.

7.3 Google AdMob

Used to display advertisements within the app. AdMob collects your Android Advertising ID and may use it for ad personalisation and measurement. You can opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings → Google → Ads. Governed by Google Privacy Policy and AdMob policies.

7.4 Google Play Services

Required for distributing and running the app on Android devices. Governed by Google Privacy Policy.

7.5 External Links

The app may display links to external websites (e.g., Google AI Studio). These external sites have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

8. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect the limited data stored locally on your device:

  • Your Gemini API key is stored using Android's encrypted SharedPreferences mechanism.
  • Communications with the Gemini API are conducted over HTTPS (TLS encryption).
  • No sensitive data is stored on external servers controlled by us.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights with respect to your data:

  • Delete local data: Clear your question history and app settings via Android Settings → Apps → InshortAI → Clear Data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Revoke microphone permission: Revoke at any time via Android Settings → Apps → InshortAI → Permissions. Text input continues to work normally.
  • Remove API key: Delete your stored Gemini API key from within the app's settings at any time.
  • Opt out of personalised ads: Go to Android Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation. You can also reset your Advertising ID from the same screen.
  • Firebase / Android ID: The Android ID is used as an anonymous identifier. You cannot change Android ID without a factory reset, but you can contact us to request deletion of your record from Firebase.
  • Google Gemini data: For questions about how Google handles queries sent to the Gemini API, refer to Google's privacy policy or contact Google directly.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app's features, legal requirements, or third-party service policies. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
