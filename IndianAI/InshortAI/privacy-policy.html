Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. Here's exactly how InshortAI handles your data.

Last Updated: September 2026
Summary: InshortAI does not collect, store, or share your questions, answers, or personal information on our servers. Your questions are sent directly to the Google Gemini API, which is governed by Google's own privacy policy. Your question history is stored only on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how InshortAI ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Mohd Rijvan Khan Mogia under the IndianAI platform, handles information when you use our mobile application ("App"). By downloading or using InshortAI, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Our app is designed with a privacy-first approach: we collect the minimum data necessary for the app to function and we do not build profiles or sell data.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

InshortAI does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use the app. The only information you voluntarily provide is:

  • Questions / Queries: Text or voice-transcribed text that you enter to ask InshortAI.
  • Google Gemini API Key: If you provide an API key, it is stored locally on your device using Android's encrypted SharedPreferences. It is never transmitted to our servers.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

We do not automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, etc.)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or crash reports (unless noted below)
  • Advertising identifiers

If a future version of the app integrates crash-reporting tools (e.g., Firebase Crashlytics), this policy will be updated and users will be notified.

2.3 Local Storage on Your Device

InshortAI stores the following locally on your device only:

  • Your question history (the questions you have asked and their answers)
  • Your Gemini API key (encrypted)
  • App settings and preferences (e.g., dark mode toggle)

This data is stored in your device's local storage and is never transmitted to our servers. You can clear this data at any time by clearing the app's data from your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

3. Google Gemini API — Third-Party Data Processing

This is an important section that you should read carefully.

InshortAI uses the Google Gemini API to generate AI responses. When you submit a question in the app, your question text is sent directly from your device to Google's Gemini API servers for processing. This means:

  • Your questions are transmitted to Google via the Gemini API.
  • Google may process, store, and use your queries in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms of service.
  • We (InshortAI / IndianAI) do not receive, intercept, or store these queries on our servers.
  • The answers generated are returned directly from Google to your device.
Important: By using InshortAI, you acknowledge and agree that your questions will be processed by Google under the Google Gemini API Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy. We strongly recommend reviewing:

• Google Privacy Policy
• Google Generative AI Terms of Service

3.1 Google's Data Handling

According to Google's documentation, data submitted to the Gemini API may be used by Google to improve their AI models, subject to their opt-out policies. The developer (InshortAI / IndianAI) has no control over Google's data processing practices. For the most current information, please refer directly to Google's privacy documentation.

4. Microphone and Voice Input

If you use the Voice Input feature, InshortAI will request access to your device's microphone. This permission is used solely to transcribe your spoken question into text. Audio is processed locally using Android's built-in speech recognition service (Google Speech-to-Text). We do not record, store, or transmit raw audio data.

You may revoke microphone permission at any time through your device's app permission settings. The app will continue to function normally using text input.

5. What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not sell your personal data to any third party.
  • We do not display third-party advertisements in the app.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, files, or other device data.
  • We do not create user profiles or engage in behavioral tracking.
  • We do not store your questions or answers on our servers.

6. Children's Privacy

InshortAI is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information to us, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate action.

Users aged 13–17 should use the app under parental supervision, particularly in relation to the Google Gemini API's own terms regarding age requirements.

7. Third-Party Links and Services

The app may display links to external websites or services (e.g., Google AI Studio for obtaining an API key). These external sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any site you visit.

8. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect the limited data stored locally on your device:

  • Your Gemini API key is stored using Android's encrypted SharedPreferences mechanism.
  • Communications with the Gemini API are conducted over HTTPS (TLS encryption).
  • No sensitive data is stored on external servers controlled by us.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights with respect to your data:

  • Delete local data: You can clear your question history and app settings by clearing the app's data in your device's Application Manager, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Revoke permissions: You can revoke microphone access at any time through your device settings.
  • Remove API key: You can delete your stored Gemini API key from within the app's settings at any time.
  • Google data: For questions about how Google handles data sent to the Gemini API, please contact Google or review their privacy policy directly.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app's features, legal requirements, or third-party service policies. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
