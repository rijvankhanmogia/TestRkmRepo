Our Mission

InshortAI was built with one clear purpose: to eliminate information overload. In a world drowning in lengthy articles, verbose search results, and multi-page explanations, we believe that knowledge should be instant, concise, and accessible.

Our mission is to empower every person — student, professional, or curious mind — to get a factual, accurate answer in the time it takes to read a single paragraph. No scrolling, no ads, no distractions.

Our Vision

We envision a world where anyone with a smartphone can access reliable, AI-generated knowledge instantly. InshortAI is designed for the next billion internet users — those who want fast, trustworthy answers without navigating complex interfaces.

By leveraging cutting-edge AI from Google and keeping the user experience razor-sharp, we aim to become the most-used quick-knowledge tool across India and beyond.

Why Exactly 60 Words?

The 60-word limit is not arbitrary — it is the result of careful research into human reading behavior and information retention. Studies in cognitive psychology suggest that the average person can absorb a complete idea comfortably in under 30 seconds of reading, which corresponds to roughly 50–70 words.

At 60 words, InshortAI's answers are long enough to be complete and informative, yet short enough to be read in a single glance. This sweet spot ensures you always get meaningful context, not just a one-liner, while still respecting your time.

The constraint also forces the AI to prioritize — choosing only the most important facts, definitions, and context. This results in cleaner, higher-quality responses than open-ended generation.

Google Gemini AI Integration

InshortAI is powered by Google Gemini — one of the most advanced large language models available today. Gemini is trained on a vast corpus of text and can understand complex questions, nuances, and context in multiple languages.

By integrating directly with the Gemini API, InshortAI ensures that answers are generated fresh for each query — not pulled from a static database. This means you always get current, contextually relevant responses tailored to exactly what you asked.

How it works: When you ask a question, the app sends it securely to the Gemini API along with a system prompt instructing Gemini to respond in 60 words or fewer. The AI processes the query and returns a concise, accurate response in seconds.

Design Philosophy

InshortAI's interface is built around one principle: get out of the way. The app opens instantly with a single input field. You ask. You read. You move on.

Every design decision — from the minimal color palette to the absence of a login screen — was made to reduce friction. We believe the best UI is the one you barely notice because everything just works.

  • No onboarding slides or tutorial pop-ups
  • No advertisements or banners
  • Dark mode for comfortable night use
  • Voice input for hands-free operation
  • Question history for easy reference
  • Copy button on every answer for quick sharing
