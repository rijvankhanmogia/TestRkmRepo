Privacy Policy

CrickAdda - Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to CrickAdda, a cricket companion app developed by Mohd Rijvan Khan Mogia under the IndianAI platform ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with the CrickAdda application ("App").

CrickAdda provides features including local cricket score tracking, live streaming links, group chat for cricket fans, and fantasy team building. Some of these features involve collecting or processing user information. Please read this policy carefully.

2. Information We May Collect

a. User-Provided Information

When you use certain features of CrickAdda, you may provide:

  • A display name or username for group chat features
  • Fantasy team selections and player preferences
  • Messages sent through the in-app group chat feature
  • Match preferences and followed teams

b. Automatically Collected Information

The App and third-party services may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers (advertising ID, device ID)
  • Device type, operating system version
  • App usage statistics and feature interaction patterns
  • Crash reports and performance data
  • General geographic location (country/region level) for regional cricket content

c. Chat Data

If you use the group chat feature, messages you send may be transmitted through third-party chat services. Please be aware that your chat messages may be visible to other users in the same group.

3. How We Use Information

Information collected may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain the App and its features
  • Enable group chat and community features
  • Support fantasy team building functionality
  • Display relevant cricket scores and match information
  • Diagnose technical issues
  • Improve the App based on usage patterns
  • Respond to user inquiries and support requests

4. Third-Party Services

CrickAdda may use third-party services including:

  • Google Play Services: Core Android functionality. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Cricket Data Providers: Match scores and schedules may be sourced from third-party cricket data services with their own privacy policies.
  • Chat Services: Group chat features may use third-party real-time messaging infrastructure.
  • Analytics Services: App usage analytics may be collected by third-party analytics providers.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third-party services.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Please be mindful that messages sent in group chat features are visible to other users in the group. Do not share sensitive personal information in chat.

6. Children's Privacy

CrickAdda is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

7. Data Retention

We retain collected information for as long as necessary to provide the App's services or as required by law. Chat messages may be retained according to the policies of the third-party chat service used. Fantasy team data is retained as long as the feature is active.

8. External Links

The App may contain links to external websites, streaming services, or cricket websites. We have no control over these external sites and are not responsible for their privacy practices. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
