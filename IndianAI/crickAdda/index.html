App Features

Everything a Cricket Fan Needs

Packed with features to keep you close to the game you love.

Live Local Scores

Follow your neighbourhood and district cricket matches with real-time scorecard updates, ball-by-ball commentary, and over-by-over analysis.

Match Streaming

Watch local cricket games live from your phone. Stream matches directly within the app and never miss a boundary or a wicket.

Group Chat for Fans

Join match-specific group chats, share your reactions, debate team selections, and connect with fans supporting the same team.

Fantasy Team Builder

Build your dream cricket XI for free! Pick players based on stats, form, and pitch conditions. Compete with friends and local leagues.
