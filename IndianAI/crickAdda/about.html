About CrickAdda

Built with passion for cricket. Designed for local fans. Free for everyone.

Our Story

Cricket Lives in Every Lane

Cricket is not just a sport in India — it is a religion. Every gully, every maidan, every neighbourhood has its own cricket heroes. But for years, local cricket had no digital home.

CrickAdda was born from that gap. We set out to build an app that brings the excitement, the camaraderie, and the stats of local and district cricket right to every fan's phone — completely free.

From live scores and match streaming to group chats and fantasy teams — CrickAdda is everything the local cricket fan deserves.

Local Cricket Focus

Dedicated to neighbourhood, club, and district-level cricket — not just national leagues.

Community First

Built around fans and their communities. Every feature is designed for connection.

Always Free

Core features including fantasy cricket and group chat are free. No paywalls.

Mobile Native

Designed from the ground up for Android smartphones. Fast, light, and reliable.

Our Mission

To democratise cricket coverage in India by giving every local match, every neighbourhood player, and every club tournament the digital spotlight it deserves — at zero cost to fans.

Our Vision

To become India's most-loved local cricket platform, where every grassroots cricketer is celebrated, every fan is connected, and every match tells a story worth sharing.
