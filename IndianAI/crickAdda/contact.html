FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CrickAdda free to download?

Yes, CrickAdda is completely free to download from the Google Play Store. Core features including live scores, group chat, and fantasy team builder are free to use.

Which devices are supported?

CrickAdda is available for Android devices running Android 6.0 (Marshmallow) and above. Download it from the Google Play Store.

Are the live scores real?

The live scores within the CrickAdda app show actual local match data (where available). Any scores shown on this website are sample/demo data only and do not represent real matches.

How is my data protected?

We take privacy seriously. Please read our Privacy Policy for full details on how your data is collected, used, and protected.
