What We Do

What We Build & How We Help

IndianAI focuses on building lightweight, fast, and practical digital tools that work well even on entry-level devices with limited internet connectivity. We believe every Indian deserves access to great technology.

Android App Development

We build native Android applications optimized for Indian network conditions and device constraints. Fast, lightweight, and data-friendly.

AI-Powered Features

Integrating modern AI capabilities like Google Gemini to provide smart, concise answers and intelligent assistance within our apps.

Offline-First Tools

All utility tools in IndianUtilityHub work completely offline — no internet needed. Financial calculators, unit converters, and more.

Privacy by Design

We design with user privacy in mind. Minimal data collection, transparent policies, and no unnecessary permissions.

Community & Social Features

Apps like CrickAdda bring communities together with group chats, shared experiences, and local content focus.

Free for All Indians

All our apps are free to download and use. We believe in making quality digital tools accessible to every Indian, regardless of income.

About IndianAI

Building India's Digital Future, One App at a Time

IndianAI is an independent app development initiative by Mohd Rijvan Khan Mogia, based in Mumbai, Maharashtra. The mission is simple: create practical, useful, and free apps that genuinely help everyday Indians in their daily lives.

Every product is designed with the Indian user in mind — apps that work on slower networks, run on modest hardware, and solve real problems that people face every day.

  • 7 apps across diverse categories
  • 100% free to use, no hidden charges
  • Offline support where possible
  • Privacy-focused design
  • Built for Indian users and Indian devices
