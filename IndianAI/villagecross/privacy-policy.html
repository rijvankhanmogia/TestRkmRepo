Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Read how VillageCross handles your information.

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to VillageCross ("we", "our", or "us"), a mobile board game developed and published by Mohd Rijvan Khan Mogia under the IndianAI Google Play Console account. This Privacy Policy explains how VillageCross handles information when you use our mobile application (the "App") or visit this website.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. VillageCross is designed as a fully offline, local multiplayer game that does not require account creation, login, or internet connectivity to function. As a result, the App collects minimal to no personal information during gameplay.

By downloading, installing, or using the VillageCross App, you agree to the terms described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

2. Information We May Collect

VillageCross is an offline game. The App itself does not require any account, login, or personal details. However, certain information may be collected in the following limited contexts:

a) Information You Provide Voluntarily (Contact Form)

If you use the contact form on our website to reach us, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number (optional)
  • The content of your message

This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry and is not shared with third parties.

b) Automatically Collected Information (Website)

When you visit our website, standard server logs or analytics tools may collect non-personally identifiable information such as:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • IP address (anonymized where possible)
  • Referring URL

c) Google Play Store

When you download VillageCross from the Google Play Store, Google may collect information per their own Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review Google's Privacy Policy separately.

3. How We Use Information

Any information we collect is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Responding to your support requests, queries, or feedback submitted via our contact form
  • Improving the VillageCross App based on feedback received
  • Diagnosing and fixing reported bugs or technical issues
  • Understanding general website traffic to improve content and user experience
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations

We do not use your information for advertising, marketing, profiling, or sale to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

VillageCross (the mobile App) does not integrate with third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or social media platforms. The App runs entirely offline.

Our website may use the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Fonts – To load the Poppins typeface. Google Fonts may log font requests. See Google Fonts Privacy FAQ.
  • jsDelivr CDN – To serve Bootstrap CSS/JS and Bootstrap Icons. jsDelivr may collect request logs. See jsDelivr Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Store – When you download VillageCross. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external services. We encourage you to read their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect any information you submit through our website. All contact form submissions are handled securely. However, please be aware that no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Since VillageCross is an offline app that stores no personal data on remote servers, the security risk from the App itself is minimal.

6. Children's Privacy

VillageCross is rated 4+ and is designed to be family-friendly. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

Since the App does not collect personal information during gameplay and requires no registration, it is safe for children to use under parental supervision. If a parent or guardian believes their child has submitted personal information via our website contact form, please contact us immediately and we will promptly delete such information.

7. Data Retention

Any information submitted via our contact form is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected — typically until your inquiry is resolved. We do not maintain long-term databases of user information.

You may request deletion of your contact information at any time by emailing us at mohdr.mogia@ril.com.

8. External Links

Our website and App may contain links to external websites (such as the Google Play Store or Google Fonts documentation). These links are provided for convenience only. We have no control over the content or privacy practices of these external sites and accept no responsibility or liability for them.

We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any website you visit via links from our platform.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights with respect to your personal data, including:

  • The right to access personal data we hold about you
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate data
  • The right to request deletion of your personal data
  • The right to object to or restrict processing of your data
  • The right to data portability

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details provided in Section 11 below.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

Your continued use of the VillageCross App or website after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
