About the Game

VillageCross is a modern digital adaptation of a beloved traditional board game played in villages and households across India. The game brings four players together on a 5×5 grid for an exciting battle of placement, strategy, and wit.

Each player places their distinctly colored cross (✕) on the grid, aiming to form lines across rows, columns, or diagonals — while simultaneously blocking opponents from doing the same. The result is a fast-paced, endlessly replayable strategic showdown.

Whether you're playing with family on a weekend or having a quick game with friends, VillageCross delivers an experience that is easy to learn but rich in strategic depth.

Meet the Developer

VillageCross was conceived, designed, and developed by Mohd Rijvan Khan Mogia, a passionate mobile developer based in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India.

With a deep appreciation for traditional Indian games and a drive to bring them to modern mobile platforms, Rijvan created VillageCross as part of the IndianAI initiative — a Google Play Console account dedicated to crafting engaging, culturally inspired games and apps for Indian users and the global audience.

The goal is simple: create fun, accessible, offline-first games that bring people together — without ads overwhelming the experience or an internet connection being a prerequisite for fun.
