2. Description of Service

VillageCross is a free, offline, local multiplayer strategy board game for Android devices. The game is played on a 5×5 grid by 4 players. Players take turns placing colored cross (✕) marks to form lines (rows, columns, or diagonals) and outlast their opponents.

Key features of the Service include:

  • 4-player local multiplayer gameplay (no online or network multiplayer)
  • 5×5 game grid with turn-based mechanics
  • Fully offline operation (no internet connection required)
  • No account creation or personal data collection within the App
