How to Play

Get started with VillageCross in just a few simple steps

1
Set Up the Board

Open VillageCross. Four players each choose a unique color — green, red, blue, or orange. The 5×5 grid starts completely empty.

2
Take Your Turn

Players take turns clockwise. On your turn, tap any empty cell on the board to place your colored cross (✕) mark in that position.

3
Form a Line to Win

Try to form a complete line with your crosses — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. Block your opponents from completing their lines!

4
Last Player Wins

When a player can no longer make valid moves or when all cells are filled, the player with the most strategic lines — or the last one with valid moves — wins!

Game Rules

Master the rules and dominate the board

Basic Rules
  • Exactly 4 players per game
  • Game is played on a 5×5 grid (25 cells)
  • Each player has a unique color: Green, Red, Blue, Orange
  • Players take turns in a fixed clockwise order
  • One cross placed per turn — no skipping allowed
  • Only empty cells may be selected
Winning Conditions
  • Form a complete row of 5 with your color
  • Form a complete column of 5 with your color
  • Form a diagonal line of 5 with your color
  • Last player with valid moves remaining wins
  • Block opponents to outlast them strategically
  • Most cells occupied can determine tiebreakers
Strategy Tips
  • Control the center — it opens diagonal opportunities
  • Block opponents who are close to completing a line
  • Build multiple lines simultaneously to force choices
  • Watch all 4 players, not just the one ahead of you
  • Corner cells are powerful — use them wisely
Game Details
  • Players: 4 (required, no AI bots)
  • Board: 5×5 grid (25 cells total)
  • Gameplay: Turn-based, offline local multiplayer
  • Average game time: 5–15 minutes
  • Age rating: 4+ (family friendly)
  • Platform: Android (Google Play)
