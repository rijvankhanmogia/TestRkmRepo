About IndianAI

The story behind the platform, our mission, and the developer building it all.

Our Mission

Why IndianAI Exists

IndianAI was born from a simple observation: while technology has advanced rapidly, truly useful, practical apps that cater to the everyday Indian user are still relatively scarce. Most apps are either too complex, too expensive, or designed for users in Western markets.

Our mission is to bridge that gap — to create free, lightweight, practical apps that work well on the kind of devices and network conditions that most Indians actually have. Not polished demos for tech conferences, but real tools for real people.

Every product in our lineup solves a specific, tangible problem: getting news quickly, creating art, playing a classic game, calculating EMIs, following local cricket, managing documents, or getting AI-powered answers. Simple. Useful. Free.

Our Core Promise

Every app we build will be free, practical, and designed with the Indian user at the center.

Our Values

What We Believe In

The principles that guide every product decision we make.

User-First Design

Every feature we build starts with a real user need. We do not add complexity for its own sake.

Privacy & Trust

Performance Matters
