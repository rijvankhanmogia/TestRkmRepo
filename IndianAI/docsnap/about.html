What is DocSnap?

DocSnap is a powerful, free Android application designed to be your all-in-one document management solution. With 21 carefully crafted tools, DocSnap handles everything from converting images to PDF, to merging, splitting, compressing, and securing your documents.

Whether you are a student scanning lecture notes, a professional handling contracts, or anyone who regularly works with PDF documents, DocSnap provides all the tools you need in one lightweight, easy-to-use Android app — completely free.

DocSnap is developed under the IndianAI platform, with a mission to make professional-grade document tools accessible to everyone.
